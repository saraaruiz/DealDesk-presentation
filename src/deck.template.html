01 Customer, user, problem and outcome

A message can look ready
and still go to the wrong person.

Customer

Partner or operations lead at a boutique advisory firm. Fewer avoidable errors, traceable decisions.

Primary user

The deal coordinator, preparing messages for several deals, fast. Jordan Reyes, invented.

Secondary user

The deal owner, Dana. Decides exceptions. The coordinator can ask, never approve.

Desired outcome

Understand the problem, act, and know if this version is ready. Never by color alone.

In the brief's sample: Sam Lee works for the client but is not on the deal team, and the draft says "Completion is guaranteed by 30 June."

The risk isn't a missing warning. It's a warning people learn to ignore.

03 An alternative, and the trade-off

Inline review, with one idea borrowed.

A · Inline review

Chosen

Issues next to the person or sentence. Denser screen, so hierarchy carries it.

B · Guided review

Step by step. Clearer sequence, but extra steps on every message.

Prevent only

Never suggest outsiders. They still arrive by paste or reply. Kept as the first layer.

DECISION

Kept A, plus a separate sheet to ask the owner. The certainty sentence blocks, it doesn't just warn. Send stays visible, off, with its reason. No "send anyway".

Mid-fidelity, black and white: states fixed before any color. My judgment, not a tested result.

06 · 07 One iteration, limits and the next research question

Ana couldn't undo a click. Now she can.

Remove Sam now shows "Sam Lee removed · Undo" with a 3-second bar. User control and freedom.

AssumptionNot tested

Remaining limits

Review, reply, send and undo are simulated. One expert walkthrough, no real coordinators. Light mode, disabled contrast and screen readers unverified.

Next research question

Which wording do owners accept?

For "Completion is guaranteed by 30 June". Could owner-written templates avoid the block?

How I'd validate

Black and white first with 3–6 people who prepare messages: a five-second test, then three tasks. Pending read as approved is a design defect. No target number.
